# Complexity

**O(n) time.** Each index is pushed exactly once and popped at most once, so the
inner `while` runs at most `n` times in total across the whole scan — at most
`2n` stack operations for the series. One pass, no re-reading.

**O(n) space** in the worst case: a series that never settles lower keeps every
day on the stack. There is no smaller bound to aim for — the answer array is
already O(n).

The naive forward scan in `rotten/main.py` is O(n²). On two of the three large
recipes every day stays open until the last session of the series, so the scan
does the full `n(n-1)/2` comparisons: about 1.25·10¹¹ at `n = 500000`. On the
third, seven days in eight stay open for good, and the scan does about
1.1·10¹¹. That is about 40 minutes in Python and roughly 30 seconds as native
code, at the repo's `-O2` or with `CFLAGS` overridden to `-O3 -march=native` —
far past the 5-second solver timeout. The single-pass version answers in about
a fifth of a second.

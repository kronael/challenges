# Complexity

**O(n) time.** Each index is pushed exactly once and popped at most once, so the
inner `while` runs at most `n` times in total across the whole scan — at most
`2n` stack operations for the series. One pass, no re-reading.

**O(n) space** in the worst case: a series that never settles lower keeps every
day on the stack. There is no smaller bound to aim for — the answer array is
already O(n).

The naive forward scan in `rotten/main.py` is O(n²). On both large recipes every
day stays open until the last session of the series, so the scan does the full
`n(n-1)/2` comparisons: about 3.1·10¹⁰ at `n = 250000`. That is close to an
hour in Python and roughly 16 seconds even as optimized native code, against a 5-second
solver timeout — and about a quarter of a second for the single-pass version.

# Hint 2

**Where does score `i` land?** At slot `k`, and `k` is how many of scores
`0..i-1` were kept. Lane `i + 1` cannot pick its address until lane `i` has
decided, so the store index is a serial chain through the whole batch, and the
vectorizer refuses it outright. Confirm it: `cc -O3 -march=x86-64-v3
-fopt-info-vec-missed -S solution.c` prints `not vectorized: unsupported use in
stmt` and stays silent about any alternative.

**The chain is only eight lanes long at a time.** Within one vector, "how many
before me" is a function of eight compare bits, and eight bits have 256 values.
Nothing about it has to be computed lane by lane at run time. What would you
need, given those eight bits, to move the kept lanes to the front of the vector
in order?

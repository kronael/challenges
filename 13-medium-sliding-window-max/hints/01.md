# Hint 1

> Spoilers. Open only when stuck, and only one file at a time.

Neighbouring windows share `k − 1` readings: one reading leaves on the left and
one arrives on the right. When the new reading is larger than some readings
already in the window, can any of those smaller, older readings still be the
maximum of a later window? What, then, do you need to keep from one window to
the next?

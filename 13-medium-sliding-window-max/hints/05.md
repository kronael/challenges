# Hint 5 — Level 3

> Spoilers for `make vec`. Levels 1 and 2 need none of them. Open only when
> stuck, and only one file at a time.

**The deque clears the bench and fails `make vec`.** How many indices a reading
pops depends on the readings before it, so eight readings cannot take the same
steps side by side. The loop is branches on values, and `make vec` grades a C
deque scalar under gcc 12 and clang 21 alike.

Look for work that is the same at every reading, whatever the values, even if
there is more of it: `make vec` counts scalar instructions per reading, not
passes over the readings. The maximum of two neighbouring readings is one such
step. What do you get if you take it again, on its own output?

# Complexity — the same filter in a different register shape

`golden/solution.c` and hint 7's form are the same block filter: `O(n)`
time and `O(n)` extra space for `pre` and `suf`, the same compares on the same
readings, the same answer. Only where the scans run differs. Hint 7's form
walks each scan a reading at a time in scalar registers; the golden one walks
it eight readings at a time and pays a few more instructions per vector for the
shifts, the masks, and the carry. The deque of hints 1–3 is `O(n)` as well.

Measured on this box — Ryzen 9 5950X, `-O3 -march=x86-64-v3`, 200000 readings,
the median of five runs, each the best of 50 calls to `solve`, in µs:

| readings, `k` | gcc `golden/` | gcc hint 7 | clang `golden/` | clang hint 7 | gcc deque |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| random, 3 | 162 | 140 | 149 | 263 | 848 |
| random, 16 | 101 | 161 | 91 | 268 | 1032 |
| random, 1237 | 100 | 174 | 92 | 143 | 1082 |
| random, 50000 | 91 | 170 | 85 | 116 | 1090 |
| falling, 50000 | 92 | 170 | 85 | 119 | 365 |
| rising, 1237 | 105 | 173 | 93 | 126 | 269 |

For `k ≥ 16`, `golden/` is 1.3 to 2.9 times faster than hint 7's form. At `k = 3`
every vector holds block starts, so every step takes the masked form, and gcc's
scalar scans win. The deque is the slowest by far on random readings, where how
many readings it pops varies from one reading to the next; on sorted readings,
where that count is predictable, it is three to four times faster.

`make vec` sees the difference without a clock. Traced on `vec.in`, 30011
readings with `k = 1237`: `golden/` retires 0.03 scalar instructions per reading
under gcc and 0.02 under clang, hint 7's form 8.03 and 7.54, and a C deque about
20.

## More work, still in lanes

Level 3 counts scalar instructions per reading, not how many readings each
window costs, but its tracer has a time limit. Swapping the loops of the
`O(n·k)` rescan — for each offset `j < k`, fold `arr[i + j]` into every
window's running maximum — gives a loop both compilers vectorize, one `vpmaxsd`
per eight windows per offset. On `vec.in` that is about `30011 × 1237 / 8`
vector steps, and `make vec` stops it: `did not finish within 60 s under the
tracer`, under gcc and clang alike. The golden traces in a few seconds.

# Approach — double the span

Let `span[i]` be the maximum of the `w` readings from `i`. At `w = 1` it is the
array itself. The maximum of the `2w` readings from `i` is
`max(span[i], span[i + w])`, so one pass over the array doubles `w`. Stop at
the largest power of two `w ≤ k`, after `⌊log₂ k⌋` passes.

A window of `k` readings is then two spans of `w` that overlap: the one from
`i` and the one from `i + k − w`. A reading counted twice does not change a
maximum, so the window's answer is `max(span[i], span[i + k − w])`.

Every pass, and the final combine, is an element-wise maximum of two arrays: no
branch on a value, and no reading waits for its neighbour's result.

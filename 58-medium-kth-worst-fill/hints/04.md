# Complexity

The naive approach — take the maximum out of the log `k - 1` times, rescanning
the remaining fills on every round, then report the maximum of what is left — is
what `rotten/main.py` does. It is O(n·k), so it is correct and passes the small
cases, but with ranks in the hundreds of thousands it TIMEOUTs on the large
cases.

Expected O(n) time per rank and O(1) extra space beyond one copy of the log; the
O(n²) worst case survives only as a vanishingly unlikely draw once the pivot is
randomized.

`make bench`'s wall-clock timeout cannot enforce this bound by itself: at
`n = 4·10⁵` a full sort is not the timeout-failing move quickselect is on the
other challenges here, so the O(n) time and O(1)-space follow-up in the README
is the real contract, not the benchmark.

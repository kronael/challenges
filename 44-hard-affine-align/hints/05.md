# Hint 5 — Level 3

> Spoilers for `make vec`. Levels 1 and 2 need none of them. Open only when
> stuck, and only one file at a time.

**Find the chain.** In the row-by-row loop of hint 2, `M[i][j]` and `X[i][j]`
read only row `i - 1`, but `Y[i][j]` reads `M`, `X`, and `Y` at `[i][j-1]`:
every cell waits for its left neighbour. Compile that loop in C with
`-O3 -march=x86-64-v3 -fopt-info-all` and gcc 12 reports that it split the row
loop in three (`Loop 4 distributed: split to 3 loops`) and ran the `M` and `X`
parts eight lanes wide, then `not vectorized, possible dependence between
data-refs` for the `Y` line. That one chain keeps the loop at about five scalar
instructions per residue pair under gcc, and about 26 under clang 21.

Which cells of the grid depend on none of each other? And if you walked the
grid a column of `t` at a time instead, which chain would be left, and could
rows far apart share one vector?

# Hint 6 — Level 3

> Spoilers for `make vec`. Levels 1 and 2 need none of them. Open only when
> stuck, and only one file at a time.

**Find the chain.** In the row-by-row loop of hint 3, `M[i][j]` and `X[i][j]`
read only row `i - 1`, but `Y[i][j]` reads `M`, `X`, and `Y` at `[i][j-1]`:
every cell waits for its left neighbour. `c/` builds with
`-fno-tree-loop-distribution`, which 44's `vec.mk` adds to every C build here,
`golden/`'s included: without it gcc 12.2 splits the row loop in three
(`Loop 4 distributed: split to 3 loops`), runs the `M` and `X` parts eight lanes
wide, and prints wrong answers. With it, `-fopt-info-all` reports
`couldn't vectorize loop` and `not vectorized, possible dependence between
data-refs` for the `Y` line. That one chain keeps the loop far over one scalar
instruction per residue pair under gcc, 10.5 on `vec.in`, and under clang 21.

Which cells of the grid depend on none of each other? And if you walked the
grid a column of `t` at a time instead, which chain would be left, and could
rows far apart share one vector?

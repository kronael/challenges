# Sources

- CLRS §15
- V. Boyer, D. El Baz, M. Elkihel, [Solving knapsack problems on
  GPU](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2011.03.014), Computers & Operations
  Research 39(1), 2012 — each item's row of the recurrence computed in parallel
  across capacities from the row before it.
- [Auto-Vectorization in LLVM — runtime checks of
  pointers](https://llvm.org/docs/Vectorizers.html#runtime-checks-of-pointers)
  — the overlap check clang puts in front of its vector loop.
- [Auto-vectorization in GCC](https://gcc.gnu.org/projects/tree-ssa/vectorization.html)
  — loop versioning for aliasing, and `-fopt-info-vec`.
- [Go `simd/archsimd`](https://pkg.go.dev/simd/archsimd) — `LoadInt64x4`,
  `BroadcastInt64x4`, `Int64x4.Greater`, `Int64x4.IfElse`.
- [Intel Intrinsics Guide](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/docs/intrinsics-guide/index.html)
  — `_mm256_cmpgt_epi64` (`vpcmpgtq`), `_mm256_blendv_pd` (`vblendvpd`), and
  `_mm256_max_epi64`, which needs AVX-512.
